Property Owners Association

HOA membership is required, not optional

Every owner of Lots 1 through 139 is automatically a member of the Sandstone Point Property Owners Association and owes its dues. The Wayne Circuit Court confirmed this in an order entered August 12, 2024.

The short version

  • Owning a lot makes you a member. There is no sign-up form and no way to opt out. Declaration §8.1
  • Dues are $200 per year. They are paid to the Association.
  • Dues stay with the lot. The dues "run with the land": they bind every later owner, heir and successor, not just the first buyer. Declaration §8.2
  • Unpaid dues follow the lot, too. They earn interest, can add collection costs and attorney fees, and become a continuing lien on the lot that the Association can foreclose. Declaration §8.6
  • A court has settled it. The Wayne Circuit Court declared that the 2005 Declaration and its Supplemental Declaration apply to Lots 1 through 139. Order, Case No. 23-CI-00307

What the court decided

Court
Wayne Circuit Court, Commonwealth of Kentucky
Case
Civil Action No. 23-CI-00307
Parties
Sandstone Point Property Owners Association, Inc. (plaintiff) v. MPD II LLC (defendant)
Entered
August 12, 2024

After hearing from the lawyers for both sides, the court ordered that:

  1. The 2022 Amendment to the Declaration (Book 404, pages 537–539) is struck from the Wayne County Clerk's records.
  2. The 2005 Declaration (Deed Book 310, pages 664–679) and the Supplemental Declaration (Deed Book 328, pages 66–68) apply to Sandstone Point Lot owners 1 through 139.
  3. The Association may ask the court to recover its attorney fees and costs under Article 15 of the 2005 Declaration.
  4. The defendant's counterclaim is dismissed.

The second page ends with the clerk's certificate of service on counsel for both parties; see the PDF.

For buyers, real estate agents and title companies

  • Title search: the 2005 Declaration is recorded with the Wayne County Clerk in Deed Book 310, page 664, and the Supplemental Declaration in Deed Book 328, page 66. Both bind Lots 1 through 139.
  • Ignore the 2022 Amendment. The court ordered it struck from the Clerk's records (Book 404, pages 537–539).
  • Tell buyers up front that the lot comes with a required Association membership and annual dues, currently $200 per year.
  • Ask the Association for a dues statement before closing. Unpaid dues are a continuing lien on the lot, so they don't go away when the lot is sold.
  • Sellers: the Declaration asks the seller to give the Association the buyer's name and mailing address after the sale (§8.8).

In the Declaration's own words

"Lot Owners shall automatically, by virtue thereof, become a member of the Sandstone Point Property Owners Association, Inc., a non-profit corporation chartered in the State of Kentucky."

2005 Declaration, Article 8, §8.1

"As a member of the Association, each Lot Owner agrees for himself or herself, and his or her heirs, successors and assigns, to pay to the Association any dues, assessments for maintenance charges and costs or fines … The Dues shall run with the land."

2005 Declaration, Article 8, §8.2

"The provisions hereof shall run with the Development and shall be binding upon all Lot Owners and all persons claiming under them for a period of forty (40) years from the date this Declaration is recorded, after which time said Declaration shall be automatically extended for successive periods of ten (10) years each …"
