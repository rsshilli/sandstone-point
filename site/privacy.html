Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026.

What we keep

  • Your login. Your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google or Facebook. If you sign in with Facebook we ask only for your name and email, nothing else from your profile. If you use a password, it is stored scrambled; nobody can read it.
  • Your lot records. The association already keeps who owns each lot, contact details owners give the board, and each lot's dues. Signing in lets you see the records for your own lots.
  • Messages you send the board through the website, which go to the board's email list.

The public pages don't use tracking or advertising cookies. Signing in keeps you signed in only until you close the browser tab.

Who sees it

The HOA board, to run the association. We never sell it or share it for advertising. The website runs on Google Cloud (Firebase), and the website's emails are sent by Resend; they handle the data only to run those services.

Deleting your data

To have your login deleted, sign in and use Tell a board member on your lot records, or ask any board member. We delete the login within 30 days and tell you when it's done. Ownership and dues records for a lot are the association's records, so they stay after a login is deleted.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Sandstone Point in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites. That stops Facebook sharing anything more with us. To remove your login here as well, ask the board as described above.

Questions

Ask any board member, or use the board links once you're signed in.
